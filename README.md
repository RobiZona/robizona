## About Me

I'm Roberto, known online as **RobiZona**, born in '93.

I've been a gamer since I was four. That passion led me to become a **YouTuber and streamer**, study **Computer Science at university**, and develop resources for multiplayer servers and external projects.

I started writing **FiveM scripts in 2019**. Over the following years, I created and ran **Inside** on FiveM for two years and **Western Legends** on RedM for four years. Both regularly hosted **100–200 players**, giving me hands-on experience with development, server management and online communities.

After years of private development for servers, I'm now focusing on **RZ**: a space to express my creativity and build **free and premium resources for RedM & FiveM**, backed by documentation and support.

Beyond game resources, I also work on **Telegram bots and Python backends**.
