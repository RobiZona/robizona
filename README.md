## The person behind RZ

I'm **Roberto**, known online as **RobiZona**, born in '93.

I've been a gamer since I was four. That passion led me to become a **YouTuber and streamer**, study **Computer Science at university**, and develop resources for multiplayer servers and external projects.

I started writing **FiveM scripts in 2019**. In the following years, I created and ran **Inside** on FiveM for two years and **Western Legends** on RedM for four years. Both regularly hosted **100–200 players**.

Running those servers gave me experience beyond writing code: maintaining live systems, managing communities and understanding what server owners and players actually need.

After years of private development for servers, I'm now focusing on **RZ** — a space to express my creativity and build **free and premium resources**, backed by documentation and support.

Beyond gaming, I also work on **Telegram bots and Python backends**.
